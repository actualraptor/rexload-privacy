Rexload privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Rexload is a workout tracker made by an independent developer for a small group of friends. This page explains what data the app handles.

Data that stays on your phone

Your workouts, sets, goals, programs, settings and profile are stored on your device. They are not sent anywhere unless you use one of the features below. You can export or delete them at any time in Settings.

The Pack (optional friends feature)

If you create a Pack account, these are stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU or US depending on the project region): your email address and password (stored securely by Supabase Auth), your display name, profile picture, motto and profile details, a summary of your training stats if you choose to show them, the posts, reactions and comments you share, friend connections, roars you send or receive, and a push-notification token if you allow notifications. Only people you accept as friends can see your profile and posts.

D.I.N.O. smart mode (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, questions you ask D.I.N.O., avatar designs you request, and a summary of your training data are sent to Anthropic to generate answers, under Anthropic's privacy policy. Your key is stored only on your phone. D.I.N.O. Lite works offline and sends nothing.

Notifications

Rest-timer reminders are scheduled on your phone. If push notifications are enabled, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers roars from friends.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Pack account → Delete my Pack account deletes your account and everything linked to it from the server immediately. Settings → Reset deletes your workout data on the phone. You can also ask for deletion by email (below).
