How to join the Rexload test

Three steps, about two minutes, on an Android phone. Use the same Google account in every step.

1Join the tester group

Open the tester group
  1. Check you're signed in with the Google account you use on your phone (top right).
  2. Tap Join group, then Join again in the pop-up.

2Become a tester

Open the test page
  1. Tap Become a tester.
  2. It should say "You are now a tester".

3Install the app

Rexload on Google Play
  1. Open the link above on your Android phone (or tap Download it on Google Play on the page from step 2).
  2. Tap Install.
  3. Keep it installed and stay in the test for at least 14 days. Updates arrive automatically, like any other app.

If it doesn't work

"App not available" or a 404 page

Wait a few minutes after joining the group (rarely up to an hour), then try again.

Still not available

You're signed in with a different Google account somewhere. Use the same one for the group, step 2 and the Play Store app (Play Store → your profile picture → switch account).

Can't find Rexload by searching

Normal for a test app. Open the link from step 3 on your phone instead.

iPhone

Rexload is Android only for now.
